# Artwork

`public/images/fried-rice-illustration.png` was created with the built-in Imagegen tool for this project and is a generated editorial illustration, not a photograph of a tested recipe. Category fallbacks are rendered locally with CSS and food emoji. Lucide supplies interface icons. Fonts load from Google Fonts with system fallbacks.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: hero food image for Myanmar Kitchen community recipe website. Create a warm, refined editorial gouache illustration of everyday Myanmar egg fried rice: a generous mound of separate golden rice grains, small scrambled egg pieces, scallions, onion and garlic in a cream ceramic shallow bowl, on a muted olive green linen table. Overhead three-quarter view, appetizing dimensional painted texture, soft natural sunlight, a simple silver spoon and small scallion sprigs nearby. Close framing with bowl in upper-middle area and calm lower margin for a caption. Palette warm cream, leafy green, terracotta and golden rice. Landscape 3:2. No text, letters, logo, borders, watermark or people. The food must visibly be fried rice, not an omelette or pasta.
